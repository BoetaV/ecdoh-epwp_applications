EPWP Advertisement for 450 General Workers

(Ages 18 – 45 years)

Applications are hereby invited for 450 EPWP General Worker positions, which will be placed across various districts within the Eastern Cape Department of Health.

Apply online Closing date: 16 November 2026 at 16h00
Applications are now closed

This is a re-advertisement

If you have already applied, you do not need to apply again. This is the opportunity for those who could not apply.

  • ContractFive (5) monthsFixed-term
  • StipendR4 500per month
  • Closing date16 November 2026Monday, 16h00

Minimum requirements

  1. A minimum qualification of Grade 10, N2 or NC(V) Level 2.
  2. A qualification in any artisan trade will be an added advantage.
  3. Applicants must submit a certified copy of their latest school report or relevant qualification certificate.
  4. Must be able to read and write.
  5. Must have a valid RSA ID document (certified copy).
  6. Must be residing in the same area/village and ward of the vacant position.
  7. Proof of residence from the Ward Councillor.
  8. Community involvement and good communication skills.
  9. Attributes: excellent time management and physical fitness to perform manual labour.
  10. Applicants must be unemployed and meet the eligibility requirements of the Expanded Public Works Programme (EPWP).
  11. Applicants must be between 18 and 45 years old.

Key responsibilities / duties

  1. Perform general cleaning and maintain cleanliness within and around health facilities.
  2. Assist with grass cutting, gardening, landscaping and maintaining facility grounds.
  3. Assist with the loading, unloading, moving and storage of materials and equipment as required.
  4. Support the maintenance and upkeep of buildings, grounds and the surrounding environment.
  5. Assist with minor repairs and renovations under supervision, where applicable.
  6. Collect and dispose of waste in accordance with health facility hygiene and waste management procedures.
  7. Assist supervisors and other staff with general duties related to the project.
  8. Perform any other reasonable duties assigned by the supervisor in support of the EPWP project.

Have this ready before you start

  • Your South African ID number (13 digits).
  • Your municipal ward number.
  • A cellphone number where you can be reached.
  • Your current home address.

Supporting documents

  • Certified copy of your RSA ID document.
  • Certified copy of your latest school report or relevant qualification certificate.
  • Proof of residence from the Ward Councillor.

You attach a clear photo or a PDF of each document in the online form.

Placements across the province

You apply under the district, local municipality and ward where you live.

  • Alfred Nzo District
  • Amathole District
  • Buffalo City Metro
  • Chris Hani District
  • Joe Gqabi District
  • Nelson Mandela Bay Metro
  • OR Tambo District
  • Sarah Baartman District
Please note
  • Only applicants who meet the minimum requirements and submit the required supporting documents will be considered.
  • Apply only once. Each ID number can apply one time.
  • Applying is free. Nobody may ask you to pay to apply or to be placed. If someone asks for money, report it to your District Office.
