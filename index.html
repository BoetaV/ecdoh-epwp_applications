EPWP Candidate Database Register for future opportunities

The Eastern Cape Department of Health is building a database of people who are available for Expanded Public Works Programme (EPWP) work at health facilities in their own ward.

Register now Free · takes about 5 minutes · works on your phone

This is not a job advert

There are no posts open right now. Registering puts your details on a database so that the Department can contact you when an EPWP opportunity becomes available in your area. Registration does not guarantee work.

  1. Register

    Fill in the online form with your details, your ward and your skills.

  2. You are on the database

    You get a reference number. Your details are kept safely by the Department.

  3. We contact you

    When an opportunity opens in your ward, people on the database can be contacted.

Who can register

  • You have a valid South African ID.
  • You are 18 years or older.
  • You live in a ward in the Eastern Cape, and can show proof of residence from your Ward Councillor if asked.
  • You are unemployed and meet the EPWP eligibility requirements.
  • You are physically fit and able to do manual labour.
  • Any school level is welcome. A trade or artisan skill is an added advantage.

Typical EPWP work

  • General cleaning in and around health facilities.
  • Grass cutting, gardening and looking after facility grounds.
  • Loading, unloading, moving and storing materials and equipment.
  • Helping with the upkeep of buildings and grounds.
  • Minor repairs and renovations under supervision.
  • Collecting and disposing of waste according to facility hygiene procedures.

Have this ready before you start

  • Your South African ID number (13 digits).
  • Your municipal ward number.
  • A cellphone number where you can be reached.
  • Your current home address.

All eight districts

You register under the district, local municipality and ward where you live.

  • Alfred Nzo District
  • Amathole District
  • Buffalo City Metro
  • Chris Hani District
  • Joe Gqabi District
  • Nelson Mandela Bay Metro
  • OR Tambo District
  • Sarah Baartman District
Important
  • Registration is free. Nobody may ask you to pay to be registered or to be placed. If someone asks for money, report it to your District Office.
  • Register only once. Each ID number can be on the database one time.
  • Being on the database is not an offer of work and does not guarantee placement.
